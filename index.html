    Sync

    Keeps your puzzles in step between the Mac and the phone, through progress.json in your private sudoku-progress repo on GitHub. It syncs on its own when you open the app and a few seconds after each move.

    Paste this device's GitHub token (Contents read and write on sudoku-progress only). It stays on this device.
